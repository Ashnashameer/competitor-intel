# EDGE – Competitor Intelligence & Market Research Hub
**An Educational Technology Initiative Backed by MYST International**

---

## 🌟 Executive Summary

**EDGE – Competitor Intelligence Hub** is a business intelligence and competitive research platform developed to analyse, monitor, compare, and benchmark competitors across the technology education, professional skill training, and EdTech landscape in India.

The platform focuses on EDGE’s core academic curriculum tracks:
- **Artificial Intelligence**
- **Generative AI**
- **Data Analytics**
- **Automation and AI Agents**
- **Python and Software Development**
- **Web and Digital Technology**
- **Emerging Technologies**

---

## 🏛️ The Five Competitor Research Segments

1. **Segment A: Schools** (K-12 schools, coding labs, Atal Tinkering Labs, robotics clubs, external vendor tie-ups)
2. **Segment B: Higher Secondary Schools** (Plus One & Plus Two Computer Science, Python & AI add-ons)
3. **Segment C: Colleges** (Engineering colleges, Arts & Science colleges, autonomous institutions, KTU finishing schools)
4. **Segment D: Independent Training & Skill Development Institutes** (Private academies, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12-month job-ready bootcamps)
5. **Segment E: Agencies, IT Companies & EdTech Providers** (Digital marketing agencies with training academies, IT software firms with internship pipelines, and EdTech platforms. *Strict Protocol: Business services are assessed separately from educational arms.*)

## 🧭 Application Navigation (14 Sections)

1. **Overview Dashboard** – Dynamic KPI cards, Kerala 14-district distribution, segment breakdown, tech matrix, and key market observations.
2. **Competitor Directory** – Centralized repository with spreadsheet data grid and profile card view.
3. **Schools** – Segment A dedicated analysis.
4. **Higher Secondary Schools** – Segment B dedicated analysis.
5. **Colleges** – Segment C dedicated analysis.
6. **Training Institutes** – Segment D dedicated analysis.
7. **Agencies & EdTech** – Segment E dedicated analysis.
8. **Competitor Comparison** – Side-by-side interactive comparison matrix across 15 technologies and commercial terms.
9. **Courses & Technologies** – Dedicated database of 35+ course syllabi, fees, and durations.
10. **Institutional Partnerships** – Confirmed MoUs vs company-reported claims vs unverified claims.
11. **Marketing Research** – Website positioning, Instagram/LinkedIn reach, workshop funnels, and placement rhetoric.
12. **Market Opportunities** – Evidence-backed gap analysis and EDGE strategic plays in Kozhikode and Kerala.
13. **Research Sources** – Source citations, dates, evidence descriptions, and verification audit trail.
14. **Geographic Research** – Territorial drilldown across all 14 Kerala districts and national state nodes.
